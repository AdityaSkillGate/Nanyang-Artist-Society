Frequently Asked Questions

Help & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Key questions regarding courses, grade examinations, test centres, and society membership.

1. How do students register for the National Grade Examination?

Candidates may register directly through accredited Singapore test centres (such as Nanyang Art HQ in Jurong East, Federation of Art Societies, AiDe Art Studio in Tampines, or Butterfly Art Studio in Jurong West), or online via our contact/registration form above.

2. Can beginners start directly at Grade 1?

Yes! Grade 1 is designed as a structured foundation level. Candidates may start at Grade 1 or undergo an entry-level assessment by society faculty if they possess prior artistic training.

3. What are the typical class sizes for studio courses?

To ensure individualized pedagogical attention and master mentorship, studio classes maintain small ratios with a maximum of 10 to 12 students per proctored class.

4. How are Nanyang Star competition submissions evaluated?

Entries are adjudicated by an esteemed international jury panel comprising senior artists, educators, and museum curators. Submissions are judged blindly across 4 age divisions according to creativity, authenticity, technical handling, and conceptual thought without formulaic copying.
