Our Pedagogical Mission

Nurturing Artistic Originality Without Formula

尊重童心本真 · 激发无限创造力

Founded under the leadership of Dr. Teng Jiashu and the Academic Council of the Singapore Nanyang Artists Society, the Nanyang Star competition was established to transcend rigid formulaic copying.

We believe a child's visual language is an authentic, profound window into the future. Each biennial edition invites young artists aged 4 to 16 to express their cultural surroundings, personal dreams, and scientific imagination through painting, traditional ink, mixed media, and patented 3D origami sculpture.
