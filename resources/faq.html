Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Key questions regarding courses, grade examinations, test centres, and society membership.

1. How do students register for the Grade Examination?

Candidates may register directly through authorized Singapore test centres (such as Nanyang Art HQ in Jurong East, Federation of Art Societies, AiDe Art Studio in Tampines, or Butterfly Art Studio in Jurong West), or online via our contact/registration portal.

2. Can beginners start directly at Grade 1?

Yes! Grade 1 is designed as a structured foundation level. Candidates may start at Grade 1 or undergo an entry level assessment by society instructors if they possess prior artistic training.

3. What age group are the studio courses suitable for?

Our programs span early childhood (from age 2 in Children's Intellectual Art) to youth and adults (Chinese Painting, Calligraphy, Academic Sketching, and Patented Plastic Origami).

4. Where is the society headquarters located?

Headquarters are located at Blk 135 Jurong Gateway Road #03-333/335, Singapore 600135, directly accessible via Jurong East MRT station (EW24/NS1) and the Jurong East Bus Interchange.
