Apex Art Alliance

Federation of Art Societies (Singapore)

新加坡美术总会

As the overarching apex body uniting Singapore's major visual art associations, the Federation of Art Societies collaborates closely with Nanyang Artists Society on academic grade examinations, exhibitions, and publications including the Singapore Art Quarterly.
