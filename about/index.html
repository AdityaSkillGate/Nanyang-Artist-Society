About Us

Our Story & Core Mission

Founded in 2002 under the proposal of pioneer master Liu Kang, cultivating Singapore's artistic future through academic rigor, heritage transmission, and international exchange.

Three Core Pillars

1. Academicism

Rigorous grounding in drawing fundamentals, brushwork mastery, color theory, and historical understanding.

2. Internationalism

Multi-cultural exchange, global competitions, international exhibitions, and ASEAN artistic collaboration.

3. Passion & Education

Stimulating creative intellect from early childhood through lifelong artistic fulfillment.

"Singapore Nanyang Artists Society was founded in 2002. Proposed and urged by pioneer artist Mr. Liu Kang, our artists have engaged in continuous academic exchanges, exhibitions, and teaching activities to nurture the third generation of Nanyang art."
