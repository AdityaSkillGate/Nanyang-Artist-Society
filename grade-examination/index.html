Progression Architecture

Grades 1 to 9 Examination Roadmap

Filter progression by examination tier.

Tier 1 · 初级段位 Grades 1, 2, 3

Foundation & Perceptual Discovery

Grip ergonomics, basic stroke controls (Zangfeng), sight-size measurements, geometric solids, and sensory color palettes.

  • Grade 1: Basic single strokes & geometric cubes
  • Grade 2: Combined geometric bodies & 4-character script
  • Grade 3: Patented 3D origami & fruit still-life groups
Tier 2 · 中级段位 Grades 4, 5, 6

Structure, Texture & Poetry Scrolls

Five tonal chiaroscuro values, multi-object still life, Tang poetry quatrains, Han stele Clerical script, and Shanshui mist.

  • Grade 4: Reflective stoneware still life & 14-char poetry
  • Grade 5: Complex glassware & 20-character Tang scrolls
  • Grade 6: Plaster facial features (David) & Four Gentlemen
Tier 3 · 高级/毕业段位 Grades 7, 8, 9

Classical Busts & Master Creation

Full classical plaster busts (Agrippa, Voltaire), live model anatomy, multi-layered Shanshui scrolls, and dual red seal harmony.

  • Grade 7: Classical plaster busts (Voltaire, Agrippa)
  • Grade 8: Plaster bust chiaroscuro & Xieyi bird-and-flower
  • Grade 9: Live model anatomy & hanging masterwork scroll
